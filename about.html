About Mamidav International Limited

One company, four disciplines, and a single standard for how work gets done.

OUR STORY

Built from one venture, grown into an integrated group

Mamidav International Limited started as a single agribusiness venture and has since grown into an integrated group spanning agriculture, power systems engineering, event management, and culinary services. What connects these different disciplines isn't convenience - it's a shared standard. Whether it's a delivery of farm produce, a switchyard installation, a wedding, or a three-tier cake, every project is carried out with the same discipline, attention to detail, and follow-through.

We built the business this way deliberately. Each division is led by someone who has spent years mastering that specific craft, so clients get specialist expertise rather than generalist effort - backed by one company that takes full accountability for the outcome, from first inquiry to final delivery.

As the group grows, so does our reach. We work with clients and partners wherever the opportunity fits, bringing the same practical, reliable execution to every engagement.
